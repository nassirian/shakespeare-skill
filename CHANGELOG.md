# Changelog

## 2.1.0 (October 2026)

- Added 18 sources on how current AI detectors work: vendor technical reports, independent benchmarks, and 2025–2026 studies of humanizers, AI-polished text and human post-editing.
- New reference `detectors.md`: what detector scores measure, what changes them honestly, where they misfire, and how to answer users who ask.
- New guidance to ask for samples of the author's own writing when matching their voice.
- Note that even light AI polish can get a human draft flagged, which reinforces minimal-edit revision.

## 2.0.0 (October 2026)

- Rebuilt from 48 sources: the original 31 detection papers plus 17 newer studies of LLM prose style, expert editing and voice homogenization.
- New workflow: plan (genre, angle, real specifics), draft, then one pattern pass.
- New references: `pattern-catalog.md` (patterns with evidence and caveats) and `fiction.md` (narrative-level patterns).
- New `scripts/pattern_check.py`, a standard-library checker for surface patterns.
- Rules against inventing small details (outcomes, settings, motives), plus bracketed prompts for facts only the author knows.
- New overcorrection section covering the tells that blind testing found when the usual ones are stripped out.
- Revision rules based on evidence that LLM "polish" passes create the AI fingerprint and flatten voice.

## 1.0.0 (September 2026)

- First version, drafted from a synthesis of 31 AI-text-detection papers.
